# Switching notice (EU Data Act)

Template generated by `kit/letter.js` for a full move from AWS before 12 January 2027. From that date, replace
point 3 with a request to confirm that no switching charges apply. The page at https://moveinfra.eu/cloud-exit-fees/
fills it in for your provider, scope and dates. CC0. Not legal advice.

```text
Subject: Notice of switching under the EU Data Act (Regulation (EU) 2023/2854)

To: Amazon Web Services
From: [company name], [registered address]
Account(s): [account, subscription or billing IDs]
Date: [date]

Dear Sir or Madam,

We hereby give notice that we wish to switch all our services, data and digital assets from Amazon Web Services to another provider, in accordance with Chapter VI (Articles 23 to 31) of Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 (the Data Act) and our agreement with you.

Please:

1. Confirm receipt of this notice and the date on which the switching process starts. We understand that the notice period may not exceed two months, followed by a transitional period of up to 30 calendar days in which you continue to provide the services and support the switch (Article 25).
2. Provide the exhaustive specification of the data and digital assets that can be exported, including metadata, and the export formats and interfaces available (Article 25 and Article 26).
3. Please tell us, itemised and before we begin, any switching charges that would apply, including data egress charges for the switch. Under Article 29, until 12 January 2027 such charges may not exceed the costs directly linked to the switching process, and from 12 January 2027 they may not be charged at all.
4. Apply your program for free data transfer out when moving off AWS, or the AWS EU Data Act Addendum to the data transfer for this move, and tell us anything you need from us for that.
5. Keep our data available for retrieval for at least 30 calendar days after the transitional period. After that period, erase all exportable data and digital assets and confirm the erasure to us in writing (Article 25).
6. Name a contact person for this switch.

Please reply to [contact name], [email].

Kind regards,

[name]
[company name]
```
